# Wrath of Herndon

## Overview
_The scores for the math exam came back, and **YOU** were the only one who failed._

Outraged and disappointed, Mrs. Herndon takes matters into her own hands and **kidnaps you**, locking you inside the school to force you to study. There's only one way out: **solve every math problem she throws at you.**

This is not just about escaping a classroom — it's about conquering the subject that once defeated you. Welcome to *Wrath of Herndon* — a math-powered escape-style game.

## Game Objective
To escape, the player must complete a series of math problems across the level of chosen difficulty:

- **Algebra II** – Solve basic equations and simplify expressions.
- **Pre-Calculus** – Tackle trigonometric identities and limits.
- **Calculus** – Analyze functions, find critical points, and evaluate integrals.

Each successful solution brings you one step closer to freedom. Can you prove your math skills and escape Mrs. Herndon's clutches?

## Gameplay
- Presented with a series of math problems.
- Enter correct answers into the provided fields.
- Problems vary in type based on difficulty: algebraic equations, limits, trigonometry, and calculus.
- Get all answers correct to unlock the ending.
- Don't get caught by Mrs. Herndon
- Spooky
> [!TIP]
> Due to how the player is programmed, moving diagonally will allow you to move faster than normal.

## Example Problems
#### Algebra II:

Solve for x: 2x + 5 = 13\
Simplify: (3x²y)(−2xy³)\
Simplify: (4x²y)(3xy²)

#### Pre-calculus:

Evaluate: limₓ→2 (x² − 4)/(x − 2)\
Solve for θ: 2cos²θ − 1 = 0 (0 ≤ θ < 2pi)\
Solve for θ: sin θ = √2/2 (0 ≤ θ < 2pi)

#### Calculus:

Integrate: ∫ (2x)/(x² + 1) dx\
Solve: d/dx ( ln(x² + 1) )\
Compute: ∫ ( 3x² dx ) from 0 to 1
> [!NOTE]
> These are just a few of the problems you can find; there are several more problems for you to solve in-game!

## Victory
“You successfully cleared all of the math problems! The Wrath of Mrs. Herndon subsides. She accepts that you have learned what you have needed to, and releases you from captivity.  
With an A on your test, you can head home with your head high, and play some video games. Or, you can stay and do some more math!”

## Installation & Running the Game

Using **Unity** and **Github**:
1. Clone the repository.
2. Open the project in Unity Hub.
3. Click **Play** to start the game.
4. Solve away!
5. Don't get caught.
> [!CAUTION]
> This project was made in Unity version **2022.3.28f1**, other versions may not run the game as well or possibly not run at all.
